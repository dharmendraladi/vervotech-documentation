Upon signing up, our Customer Success Team will guide you through the onboarding process. You will receive access to the following essential components for integration:

**1. API Credentials**
Your accountId and apiKey, required for making authorized requests to the Vervotech Mappings APIs. These credentials should be stored securely and used in all API calls.

**2. FTP Server Account**
A secure FTP account for uploading bulk content files and downloading reports related to your mapped and unmapped content. Ideal for handling large-scale data transfers and regular content updates.

**3. Vervotech Mappings Portal**
A centralized web portal that allows you to analyze your content, manage provider data, and upload your own properties to your Vervotech Mappings account.

**4. Customer Success Portal**
A dedicated support hub offering onboarding materials, integration videos, API usage guidelines, and best practices to ensure a smooth and successful implementation.
